SECTION 01

Executive Summary

Disaster situations involve multiple affected zones, incomplete and rapidly changing information, scarce food/medical/shelter/rescue resources, multiple operating agencies, and high risk of duplicated or misdirected effort.

⚠️ SYSTEM ARCHITECTURAL MANDATE

This prototype uses deterministic rule-based modules and does not use an external LLM.
All decisions, scoring, allocations, and explanations are produced through verifiable, explainable mathematical formulas and discrete logic tables.

SECTION 02

Problem Statement

Traditional emergency operations centers face compounding friction points when synchronizing response across independent agencies during severe disasters.

⏳

Information Delay

Field conditions fluctuate minute-by-minute while communications remain fragmented across radios and paper logs.

📉

Resource Scarcity

Multiple zones compete simultaneously for limited blood plasma, trauma kits, search equipment, and relief food.

👥

Manual Coordination

Different NGOs, military units, and state agencies deploy independently without a shared real-time ledger.

❓

Priority Ambiguity

Without a mathematically grounded triage formula, critical high-casualty sectors risk being deprioritized.

🔁

Duplicate Effort

Two agencies inadvertently deliver the same supplies to one zone while an adjacent zone remains completely unserved.

🌪️

Changing Conditions

A dam breach or aftershock suddenly shifts the epicenter, requiring immediate pipeline reallocation without manual recomputation.

Why Existing Manual Workflows Struggle

Manual dispatch relies on phone calls, ad-hoc spreadsheets, and verbal briefing sessions. In dynamic disasters, human coordinators cannot track multi-factor priority weights, calculate remaining stockpile percentages, or detect cross-agency duplicates before trucks depart depots.

SECTION 12

Priority Scoring Engine

The official, mathematically rigorous PS20 PRD formula implemented in RESQ-AGENT:

OFFICIAL PS20 PRD FORMULA SPECIFICATION
Priority Score = 0.40 × CasualtyScore + 0.25 × PopulationScore + 0.20 × DamageScore + 0.15 × TimeSinceLastAidScore

Final Score is bounded strictly between 0.0 and 100.0.

1. CasualtyScore (Weight: 40%)

Normalized against the maximum casualties seen in the current active batch/cycle:
min(100, (casualties / max_casualties_seen) × 100)
If max casualties is 0, score is strictly 0.0.

2. PopulationScore (Weight: 25%)

Normalized against a regional scale benchmark of 10,000 residents:
min(100, (population / 10,000) × 100)
Capped at 100.0 for dense urban zones.

3. DamageScore (Weight: 20%)

Discrete infrastructure destruction mapping:
• LOW = 20.0
• MEDIUM = 60.0
• HIGH = 100.0

4. TimeSinceLastAidScore (Weight: 15%)

Escalates priority for zones suffering prolonged neglect:
min(100, hours_since_last_aid × 5)
Reaches maximum urgency cap of 100.0 after 20 hours unserved.

SECTION 13

Resource Allocation Engine

The official deterministic allocation algorithm dispatches scarce resources in strict priority order under inventory protection caps:

Step Algorithm Operation Formula / Constraint
1 Priority Sorting Sort active operational zones descending by Priority Score.
2 Greedy Capped 40% Allocation allocate = min(units_requested, 0.40 × available_units, remaining_need)
3 Stock Decrement available_units = available_units - allocate (Guaranteed Zero Floor)
4 Ledger Commitment Generate Allocation row with rule_triggered = 'greedy-capped-40pct'
5 Escalation Check If remaining_need > 0, raise automated ESCALATION alert.
6 Agency Assignment Assign responding agency in round-robin order across registered services.

SECTION 26

Limitations & Risks

Explicit PS20 hackathon non-goals and controlled operational boundaries:

No Live GIS / GPS Feeds

The prototype models geographic sectors via discrete Zone IDs rather than live vector map tiles or satellite coordinates.

No Real Telecom Ingestion

Does not integrate live SMS, WhatsApp, or voice-radio transcription gateways; relies on structured report payloads.

No Multi-Agency Auth

Production authentication (OAuth/SAML) is mocked via agency identifier tags for hackathon demonstration clarity.

No Free-Text NLP / LLM

Avoids stochastic language models to guarantee zero hallucination, sub-second latency, and 100% mathematical auditability.

Controlled Prototype Scale

Optimized for regional operations centers handling dozens of disaster zones rather than nationwide concurrency.

Deterministic Heuristics

Allocation rules use transparent greedy 40% caps rather than complex NP-hard mixed-integer linear programming solvers.
